FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 10/15/26


Felt like we went back to summer earlier today with the warm temperatures, but it cooled off pretty quickly this evening. Today we heard of quite few small spot being caught on both the north and south beaches, and there were some small black drum reported in Frisco this afternoon.

Shane went out with Captain Del Monte (Retreiver Charters) the other day and caught a nice red drum! Looks like it was a pretty day on the water! Thanks for sharing!
