FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 9/17/26


Seems like there was a pretty decent bite earlier! We had reports today of pompano and sea mullet scattered around, and on the south beaches there were blues, sharks, red drum, and jacks being caught. Emily and Ryan caught needlefish, jacks, and more down in Hatteras today, and Ronnie had a good haul of sea mullet and some blues today as well! Great work, everyone! Thanks for sharing!
