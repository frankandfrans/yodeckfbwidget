FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 9/16/26


Feels like the cooler weather kind of disappeared during the day today! It got warm again! This morning we had reports of a couple of red drum at the point, and throughout the day there were some blues, small pompano, and a few sea mullet. Captain Scarborough (Speck-Tackler) and his guests got into some nice blues and puppy drum, and Captain Van Dyke (Fingeance Sportfishing) took out Andy and Bob, and they caught some nice bluefish around 24", and a 43.5" red.
Cliff caught a 35" red on the pier last night, and Dave had a 35" red off the point this morning. Ronnie had a citation whiting weighing 1.5lbs and a small pompano. Nice work, everyone! We got in some more beautiful finger mullet this morning, and have cobs and bigger mullet, too! Come see us and grab some!
