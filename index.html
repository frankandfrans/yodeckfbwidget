FRANK AND FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT - 9/15/2026


If the weather were like today for a while, we wouldn't complain! Drum are showing up! Several were caught off the piers today. The beach has produced some blues, croaker, and a few small pompano and sea mullet today.

Capt. Rick's crew caught some grey trout, a ribbon fish, and a couple of bluefish. Derek is catching blues on the beach.

The NE isn't stopping now! It will continue through tomorrow and some of Thursday, then we will have a light and variable afternoon. It took a good bit to hold out there today. We have pyramid sinkers all the way up to 14oz as well as sputniks up to 8oz! You shouldn't need that much, but we have a wide variety. Stop by 6am-9pm! #fishingreport #hookedonfandf
