FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 10/16/26


As dismal an evening as it's been, maybe a fish picture will make us all smile! David caught a 40" drum a few days ago at ramp 27! Pretty work! We heard of some smaller fish being caught today on both northern and southern beaches again, such as hand-sized pompano, spot, and small flounder.
