FRANK AND FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT - 9/22/2026


The wind is starting to pick up and will keep getting stronger as we head into tomorrow. If you're headed this way this weekend, keep an eye on NCDOT NC 12 for updates on any road closures.

Bluefish all over today. We heard of blues on the beach today, specifically at ramp 32. Becky, Dino, Tater, and Jeff did well on the boat, and Capt Rick had a good day with blues and a few trout. There was also a decent bite of drum at the point this morning.

The rain just started here, and it looks like it'll be on and off for a good bit. We are still here 6am-9pm so if you don't brave the weather, you can always shop! #fishingreport #hookedonfandf
