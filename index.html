FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 8/13/26


We had pretty weather today, but we hear the biting flies have been pretty bad on the beach. Some folks braved them and caught a few Spanish and croaker, as we heard, but there wasn't much else.

The inshore charters have been doing pretty well recently; Captain Frank Hopkins (Easy Going Charters) has been having luck putting people on to some nice red drum, like the 44" featured here! Captain Rick Scarborough (Speck-Tackler Charters) and Captain Joey VanDyke (Fingeance Sportfishing) have been finding a lot of bluefish, and Buddy took out Marliana, Andy, Drew, and Anna for some nice reds, including Marliana's 47 incher and Andy, Drew, and Anna's mid-slot drum, all ranging from 22-24". Pretty work, everyone!
