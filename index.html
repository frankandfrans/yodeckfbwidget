FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 8/21/26


We had some storms this morning, but it turned out to be a pretty day for the most part. A little more rain has moved in this evening.

The Spanish are back at it again, running up and down the beach. Leah and Brantley caught several nice ones today, and Zoey caught a nice one, too! Captain Scarborough (Speck-Tackler Charters) took out the Barwicks for some Spanish and blues, and JJ caught a nice blue down at Ramp 55. Pretty work, everyone!

Another week has come and gone; for everyone heading home, thank you for coming, and we'll see you next time! For those starting their week next week, don't forget to stop in for your fishing license, ORV self-certification card to drive out on the beach, and anything else you may need to have a fun week on the island!
We are open daily from 6am-9pm, and you can follow us and shop online at www.hatteras-island.com
