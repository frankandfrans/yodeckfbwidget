FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 10/1/26


Happy October! We started off the month really well, with visitors coming back to the island and reports of a good bluefish bite at the point earlier, and some scattered pompano on the beachside. Soundside has produced some speckled trout, bluefish, and red drum. Brian sent in a picture of Kristin with a nice red drum, and Beth got into the blues today here in Avon, catching a 21" and a 19". Rick caught a 25" speck on the sound side today. Way to go, everyone! Thanks for sharing!
