FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 10/3/26


We started off the day with sun, but ended it with some much-needed rain. Today we have had reports of small pompano scattered around, and there have been some blues and red drum being caught on the south beaches. Robert had a nice variety of fish the other day: pompano, blues, and sea mullet! And last night, Christie caught her first drum at the point, a 29"! Nice work!
