FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 8/5/26


The heat is still on, but at least we didn't have any rain down here today. We heard of some blues around Avon today, and some scattered spot, croaker, and small flounder and pompano.
Dusty caught and released a flounder and small pompano, and Ethan sent in a picture of a shark he caught last week off of frozen bonita from here at the shop. Alecia sent us a picture of Jack's first fish, saying, "This is Wayne and Jack. This is Jack's first time fishing and very first catch. He is 2 years old. Speckled trout caught on the sound in Avon." Well done, buddy! Haley sent in a photo of Brandon with a nice blue he caught yesterday just south of the Avon Pier, and Captain Scarborough of the Speck-Tackler took out a charter for fish and clams, and came back with a nice haul for both!
