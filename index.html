FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 8/14/26


It's been another hot one, with some biting flies still around on the beaches, but they haven't been as bad further south, from what we've been told. We heard of some Spanish in Avon this morning, and some sea mullet and croaker and a few flounder scattered around the island. The Sadowski's have had a good week of fishing, catching blues, flounder, croaker, sea mullet, and pompano. Captain Scarborough (Speck-Tackler) took his charter out for a nice haul of blues and Spanish, and Matt and his buddy pulled in a nice shark. Nice catches, everyone! Thanks for sharing!
