FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 7/31/26


It's been gorgeous out today, and we have reports of some sea mullet and bluefish scattered around on the ocean side, and soundside there are some blues and sheepshead. Brady and Henry caught some blues and sea mullet, and Eli and Chase caught some sea mullet and spot this week, too! Tom has been catching sea mullet as well, and a beautiful northern kingfish! Pretty work, everyone!

Another load of fresh mullet came in today to stock us up for the weekend; hopefully we will hear of some more fish action in the next few days!
