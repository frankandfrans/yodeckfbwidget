FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT AND WEEKLY KIDS' TOURNAMENT RESULTS!


We will get to the fishing report, but first, the winners of the Weekly Kids' Tournament! The winner this week for the 7-year-old and younger age group is Cooper (3) with his 13" bluefish! His family said he loved reeling it in, but was a bit afraid to stand behind it for the picture; we don't blame you, Cooper! Look at those teeth! Great catch, buddy!
Winning for the 8-12 year old age group is Lucas (10), with his 11" sea mullet! Nice catch, bud! Thank you guys for participating!

We heard of the typical smaller fish today, such as sea mullet and croaker up and down the beach, but there were also some bluefish scattered around out there, as well. We got in a load of fresh mullet for bottom fishing bait today, and we restocked again on more sting silvers for the bluefish and Spanish mackerel. Stop in and see us!
