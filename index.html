FRANK & FRANS HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 11/20/26


Another day with LOTS of sea mullet and puppy drum reported from the northern beaches down to Buxton. Ramp 34 sounds like it's been a hot spot. There were also some small flounder and blowtoads mixed in.

Dustin caught a puppy drum on Ramp 34 with his new setup from our store, and Gary caught a 20" puppy while wearing his lucky Frank & Frans shirt! He says, "I caught this 20" slot red Wednesday wearing my self-proclaimed 'Lucky' Frank & Fran's shirt. The only time I catch a decent drum is wearing this shirt. One photo is about 20 years old and one is 11 years old.
There are constantly evolving rules on the use of said shirt to preserve the mojo."
Nice! We love to see it!
Mike "Mullet Man" Creech caught another mess of sea mullet today, and a couple of puppy drum, a 26.75" and a 26.5"
Way to go, everyone!
