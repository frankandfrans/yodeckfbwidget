One of the coolest parts of owning a tackle shop is watching this life cycle happen right in front of us.


We see kids come through the doors for their first rod, their first rig and their first fishing trip. Before long, they’re picking out their own gear, bringing in photos of their catches and sharing the same excitement with their children and grandchildren.

We have owned the business for ten years and we’ve had the privilege of watching generations of anglers walk through Frank & Fran’s—each with their own stories, favorite fishing spots and memories made along the beaches of Hatteras Island.

Fishing isn’t just a hobby. It’s a tradition passed down one cast, one fish and one early morning at a time. And we’re grateful to be a small part of that journey.
