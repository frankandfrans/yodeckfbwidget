FRANK AND FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT - 10/4/2026


Very cloudy out today, but the temps aren't too bad. Looks like some thunderstorms are possible tonight and in the morning, but dry weather for the remainder of the week after that. We have heard of blues, spot, pompano, sea mullet, speckled trout, and a few black drum today.

Capt Rick and his crew did well on the blues and drum. Brian has been enjoying catching some speckled trout and sea mullet. And yesterday, Beth caught some pompano and black drum in Avon. Pretty work, y'all!

We have lots of fresh cob mullet left, but we are out of fingers. As a reminder, the commercial fisherman cannot catch mullet on Saturdays, Sundays, or Mondays. So the soonest we will have more fresh fingers is Tuesday, but we have plenty of nice fresh cobs and frozen finger mullet in stock.
