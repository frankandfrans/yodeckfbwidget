FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 10/9/26


Happy Friday! We have reports today of quite a few things biting, and not just the mosquitoes! Last night in Buxton and in Frisco there was a red drum bite, and today we heard of some bluefish and flounder on both the north and south beaches, and small pompano down south. Joe caught a pompano today on ramp 55, and Wendy sent in a picture of Brad with a bluefish at the point this morning, and Clayton and Curtis caught some red drum there as well. Zach caught a nice blue, and last night at ramp 49, Aaron and Ashley caught some nice red drum, Aaron with a 44", and Ashley with a 34". Great catches, everyone!
