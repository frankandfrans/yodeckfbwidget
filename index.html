FRANK AND FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT - 8/4/2026


We kept saying we needed rain... needed rain... needed rain... and now, we are just hoping the rain will stop! Another heavy downpour last night caused a decent amount of flooding on the road, especially in Salvo and between the Pea Island visitor center and the bridge. If you are travelling through it, be cautious, go slow, or avoid it if you can!

This time, we are not tricking you with a nice drum... because this one was caught today! Reports have slowly come in throughout the day. Lots of croaker, some spot and sea mullet, but Mia got the catch of the day with this beauty of a drum across the street here in Avon! And... the bait of choice was year-old Fishbites paired with a sand flea.

We loaded up with fresh mullet this afternoon. They are about 1 lb- 1.5 lb in size and nice and fresh! We also stocked up on all other baits, including bonita, today. Come see us 6am-9pm. #fishingreport #hookedonfandf
