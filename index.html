FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 8/6/26


It's been pretty breezy today, and we have heard the surf is stirred up, but there are still reports of small sea mullet, croaker, flounder, and pompano on the ocean side (as well as sharks!) Soundside has been producing some red drum at night and blues during the day. Captain Frank Hopkins (Easy Going Charters) took Drew out fishing, and Drew caught a 36" red drum. Luke brought in a citation sea mullet weighing 1 lb 14 oz, and he had a nice haul of some blues and pompano this week. Pretty work, guys!
