The sea mullet are here on Hatteras Island! 🎣 If you’ve ever felt one put a bend in a light rod, you know why we love the fall run. And with Thanksgiving coming up, who’s up for a fish fry? 🍽️


Ever wondered which kind you reeled in? Around here we call them sea mullet, but you might know them as whiting or kingfish. Our graphic shows how to tell Southern, Northern, and Gulf kingfish apart. Here’s a tip for your next trip: try a short cast into the trough just inside the first line of breakers. Those fish may be feeding closer than you think!
