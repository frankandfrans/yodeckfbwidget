FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 8/7/26


Happy Friday! We've had a beautiful day to get ready to start off the weekend, with reports of sea mullet on the north end of the island, and smaller pompano scattered around down south. There are still some reds and blues being caught soundside. Rick sent in a photo of Mike with a nice 31" red drum yesterday on the sound, and Luke came in again today with yet another citation sea mullet: same weight as yesterday's at 1 lb 14 oz, but this was a bit bigger, being 17.25" long. Nice catches, guys! Thanks for sharing! We're stocked up with some nice fresh mullet for the weekend, and have lots of other baits as well, such as frozen squid, clams, bonita, sand fleas, and more.
