FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 9/18/26


Fishing was a bit slower today, but we did hear of some drum down in Frisco at ramp 49 and some Spanish this morning in Buxton. There's still some Jack Cravalle being caught as well, mixed in with the Spanish. Captain Joey Van Dyke (Fingeance Sportfishing Charters) took out his guests and caught lots of Spanish, blues, and jacks. Nice work!
