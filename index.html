FRANK AND FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT - 10/27/26


Some good news was released by NCDOT NC 12 not long ago: they plan to have a one-lane detour open by Friday around noon. However, they did not specify whether visitors will be allowed on at that time. Dare County Government said "A determination regarding visitor reentry will be made by Dare County officials once NCDOT has a firm date for reopening the one lane road. We will share an update as soon as a decision is made." This gives us some hope!

Fishing has been decent for those who are out there: puppy drum and a few yearlings as well. It definitely was a beautiful day for it! Congrats, guys!

Right now, we are still 7 a.m.- 5 p.m., and we will let everyone know when/if that changes when everything opens back up! For now, thank you to all the ferry, NCDOT, law enforcement, EMS, etc., who have to deal with all the changes and challenges that the island has been facing! #fishingreport #hookedonfandf
