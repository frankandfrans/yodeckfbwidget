FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 10/12/26


It's been an absolutely beautiful day, despite the wind. Those who fished in the wind reported small sea mullet, bluefish, and puppy drum on the beach side from Avon and Buxton. On the sound side, there were some small speckled trout being caught.

Brandon sent some pictures of a 48" red drum he caught at the point a few days ago, and Emily sent in some pictures of her crew catching some 42" and 47" red drum while fishing on Red Finn Charters. Tracey sent us a picture of Paul's 24" bluefish at ramp 38 today. Mike came in and got a new rod and reel from us, then went out and caught a 25" puppy drum. Yesterday, Patrick brought in a citation sea mullet, weighing 1lb and 9oz, and tonight, Oliver caught a 31" drum in Buxton. Nice catches, everyone! Thank you for sharing!

Besides mullet, drum also love to bite on tuna belly! We have frozen tuna belly in stock, so come and grab some and head out to catch a big one! We are open daily from 6am-9pm, and you can follow us online at www.hatteras-island.com
