FRANK AND FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT - 11/3/2026


The beautiful weather has continued, and fish are being caught! Sea mullet, blues, drum, a few blow toads, and black drum have been reported today. We weighed in a couple of citation mullet today.
We also loaded up on fresh mullet today, a bit smaller than what we had this weekend... whew! 800lbs came in today, and hopefully we'll be getting more tomorrow! We are ready for all of the anglers for the tournament and look forward to seeing all of you. Open 6am-7pm daily!
