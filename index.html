FRANK & FRAN'S HATTERAS ISLAND FISHING REPORT 9/11/26


It's been a busy fishing day, with reports of several species being caught, including bluefish, Spanish, sea mullet, and pompano, but we're hearing that the fish are on the smaller side. It sounds like cut mullet has been the most effective bait, and we have plenty of finger mullet and larger ones.

Captain Frank Hopkins (Easy Going Charters) and Rick had a great night of catching red drum, including a 47"! Rick also caught a 37" and a 35". Buddy took out Angie, Kat, Noele, and Lauren, and they all caught nice reds ranging from 24"-33" and some bluefish. Nice work, everyone!
