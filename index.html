🤡 DON’T BE A CLOWN—AIR DOWN! 🛞🏖️


We’ve had lots of questions this week about airing down for beach driving on Hatteras Island, so we put together a few tips to help you stay on top of the sand—and out of it!

✅ Air down to 18–20 PSI
✅ Air down before entering the ramp
✅ Avoid spinning your tires if you lose momentum
✅ Air back up before returning to highway speeds

Want to make the process faster? Stop by Frank & Fran’s and check out our J.T. Brooks Tire Deflators—four different models available!

When you’re ready to leave the island, use the FREE AIR on the north side of the Frank & Fran’s building to bring your tires back to the recommended highway pressure.
